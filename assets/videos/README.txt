===================================================================
MOCK INTERVIEW VIDEO FOLDER - SEAN ADREI CUBIO PORTFOLIO
===================================================================

To display your mock interview video on your website:

1. Save your recorded video file into this folder as:
   assets/videos/mock_interview.mp4

   (Supported formats: .mp4, .webm)

2. Your portfolio website (index.php) will automatically detect
   and play the video in the Mock Interview section with full
   controls, interactive timestamps, and HD resolution!

3. Alternatively, if you want to embed a YouTube or Google Drive
   video instead, you can simply paste your embed iframe into the
   designated spot in index.php (inside the #interview section).

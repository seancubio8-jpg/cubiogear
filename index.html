My Reflection

What I have learned during my IT journey.

As an Information Technology student, I have learned that developing a website or system requires more than writing code. It also involves planning, problem-solving, testing, and understanding the needs of users.

Through my academic projects, I have experienced that mistakes are part of learning. When something does not work, I need to check the code, identify the possible cause, and try different solutions. These experiences help me become more patient and careful when working on technical tasks.

I also learned the importance of organizing information and designing interfaces that users can understand. A system should not only work properly; it should also be easy to use and useful for its intended purpose.

In the future, I want to improve my programming skills, learn more development tools, and gain experience by working on projects. I understand that technology keeps changing, so I need to continue learning and practicing.
